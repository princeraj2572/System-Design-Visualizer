# CLAUDE.md

## Commit rules

- **Subject line:** imperative mood, sentence case, no trailing period
  (e.g. `Add smart alignment guides and snapping while dragging nodes`).
- **Leading verb:** start with a plain verb such as Add, Fix, Make, Restyle,
  Redesign, Center. Do not use Conventional Commit prefixes (`feat:`, `fix:`)
  or scopes.
- **Describe the user-facing change**, naming the inspiration where relevant
  (e.g. "Eraser-style", "Figma/Eraser-style").
- **One feature or fix per commit.** Closely related items may be grouped
  (e.g. `Add X, Y, and Z to the palette`).
- **Body (optional):** wrapped prose explaining what changed and where the
  logic lives.
- **No AI attribution:** never add a `Co-Authored-By: Claude …` trailer or any
  "Generated with Claude Code" line to commits or pull requests.
